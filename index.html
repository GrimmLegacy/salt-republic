The Drowned
Serenissima

The water reached the fourth step in 1494 and has risen a finger-width every year since. Venice did not drown all at once: it is still there, half under, still working, still keeping records of a year nobody admits to.

  • ⌖

    Four realms under the lagoon

    A clockwork belfry above the clouds, the Doge's flooded archive, a hydroponics farm eight hundred fathoms down, and a railway to the stars. Each has its own work, its own people and its own price.

  • ◈

    A deck instead of a dialogue wheel

    Every action draws from the tide deck. A passing card changes the board; a failing one is a malus that follows you until you clear it. Your cards are never taken away.

  • ▧

    Every choice is written down

    The chronicle records what you did and why. Pass an encounter and its page closes; fail and it stays in its realm, waiting for a better attempt. The lore opens one chapter at a time, and never in advance.

  • ◷

    It runs while you are away

    Vigor and the card reserve refill on the clock, not while you watch. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, and the count is done: nothing was lost and nothing was wasted.
